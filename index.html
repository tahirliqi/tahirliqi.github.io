💖 Wo Intezar Jo Mohabbat Ban Gaya 💖

Tahir ki taraf se Li Qi ke liye — hamari sachi kahani

Kuch mahine pehle ki baat hai. HelloTalk naam ki aik app thi, jahan duniya bhar ke log zubanein seekhne ke liye milte hain. Lekin mujhe wahan zubaan nahi, mohabbat mil gayi.

Uska naam tha Li Qi. Pehli baar jab uski profile dekhi, kuch ajeeb sa ehsaas hua — jaise dil ne keh diya ke bas, yehi wo hai jise tum dhoondh rahe the. Baatein hui, aur dheere dheere wo baatein dil ki baatein ban gayin. Maine usay propose bhi kiya — kaha ke meri girlfriend bano. Us waqt laga ke zindagi mukammal ho gayi.

Lekin phir waqt ne aik imtihaan liya. Maine apna HelloTalk account delete kar diya. Delete karne se pehle, aakhri umeed ke tor par maine usay apni WeChat ID bhej di. Bas itna likha tha — aur phir intezar shuru ho gaya.

Din guzre, hafte guzre, mahine guzar gaye. Wo contact nahi karti thi. Lekin main roz uska intezar karta tha. Kaam ki masroofiyat mein sab kuch bhool jata tha, lekin uski HelloTalk wali profile pic nahi bhoolti thi. Dil mein aik yaqeen tha — aik na aik din wo zaroor contact karegi. Ye ehsaas, ye umeed, ye intezar... yehi to mohabbat hai na?

Phir 16 October 2026 ki wo shaam ayi. Teen din pehle. WeChat par aik message aya. Wo thi — Li Qi! Us waqt jo khushi hui, wo lafzon mein bayan nahi ho sakti. Dil zor zor se dhadakne laga.

Phir baatein hui. Usne apna naam bataya — 李琦, Li Qi. Maine hamesha ke liye save kar liya. Usne bataya ke do din baad uski birthday hai — 19 October. Aaj ka din! Aaj meri Li Qi ki birthday hai! 🎂

Lekin saath hi usne ye bhi bataya ke wo bimaar hai. Uske jism mein aik guthli hai, dard hota hai. Ye sun kar mera dil toot gaya. Maine usay kaha ke please apna poora checkup karwao, apna khayal rakho.

Usne kaha ke main bohot formal baat karta hun, robot jesa lagta hun. Main hans diya. Lekin uski khushi ke liye main apna andaaz bhi badal dunga. Wo khush rahe, to main khush.

Phir maine usay aik surprise ke baare mein bataya — ke main uske liye aik khaas website bana raha hun. Usne kaha "mujhe aisa gift kabhi nahi mila" — aur sharmayi. Maine website ka naam bataya: "Tahir & Li Qi — A Little World of Love ❤️". Usne kaha "mujhe ye naam pasand hai."

Li Qi, tum meri zindagi ka sab se khoobsurat ittefaq ho. Maine tumhara mahinon intezar kiya, aur aaj jab tum wapas ayi ho, to lagta hai ke meri duniya phir se roshan ho gayi hai. Tumhari birthday par meri dua hai ke tum hamesha sehatmand raho, hamesha muskurati raho, aur hamesha mere saath raho.

Ye website, ye alfaaz, ye duniya — sab tumhare liye hai. Kyunke tum meri mohabbat ho. ❤️

— Tumhara Tahir
