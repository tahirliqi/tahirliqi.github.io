塔希尔写给李琪 —— 我们真实的故事

From Tahir to Li Qi — our true story

那是几个月前的事了。有一个叫 HelloTalk 的应用，世界各地的人在那里学习语言。但我在那里学到的不是语言，而是爱情。

她的名字叫李琦。第一次看到她的资料时，心里有一种奇怪的感觉——好像心在告诉我：就是她，这就是你要找的人。我们聊了起来，慢慢地，那些聊天变成了心里话。我还向她表白了——我说，做我的女朋友吧。那一刻，我觉得人生圆满了。

但后来，时间给了我一个考验。我删除了我的 HelloTalk 账号。在删除之前，作为最后的希望，我把我的微信号发给了她。只写了那几个字——然后，等待开始了。

日子一天天过去，几个星期过去了，几个月过去了。她没有联系我。但我每天都在等她。忙工作的时候，什么都忘了，却忘不了她在 HelloTalk 上的头像。心里有一个信念——总有一天，她一定会联系我。这种感觉，这种希望，这种等待……这不就是爱情吗？

然后，2026年10月16日的那个傍晚到了。三天前。微信上收到一条消息。是她——李琦！那一刻的喜悦，无法用语言形容。心跳得那么快。

我们聊了起来。她告诉我她的名字——李琦。我永远地记在了心里。她说两天后是她的生日——10月19日。两天后，那个美丽的日子来了——我的李琪的生日！那一天，我为她建造了这个爱情小世界。🎂

但她同时也告诉我，她生病了。身体里长了个包，会疼。听到这个，我的心碎了。我对她说，请一定要去做全面的检查，好好照顾自己。

她说我聊天太正式了，像机器人一样。我笑了。我说，为了你，我会一直认真，因为你在我心里很重要。但为了让你开心，我也会改变我的方式。你开心，我就开心。

然后我告诉她一个惊喜——我在为她做一个特别的网站，里面写满我对她的爱和想说的话。她说"我还从来没收到过这样的礼物呢"——还害羞了。我告诉她网站的名字："Tahir & Li Qi — A Little World of Love ❤️"。她说"我挺喜欢这个名字的。"

李琦，你是我生命中最美丽的意外。我等了你几个月，如今你回来了，感觉我的世界又亮了起来。我的愿望，今天和永远都一样：愿你永远健康，永远微笑，永远在我身边。

这个网站，这些文字，这个世界——都是为了你。因为，你就是我的爱情。❤️

— 你的塔希尔

This was a few months ago. There was an app called HelloTalk, where people from all over the world meet to learn languages. But I didn't find a language there — I found love.

Her name was Li Qi. The first time I saw her profile, I felt something strange — as if my heart was telling me: this is her, this is the one you've been looking for. We started talking, and slowly those chats became heart-to-heart talks. I even confessed to her — I said, be my girlfriend. In that moment, life felt complete.

But then, time gave me a test. I deleted my HelloTalk account. Before deleting, as a last hope, I sent her my WeChat ID. Just those few words — and then, the waiting began.

Days passed, weeks passed, months passed. She didn't contact me. But I waited for her every day. Busy with work, I forgot everything — except her HelloTalk profile picture. There was a belief in my heart — one day, she would definitely contact me. This feeling, this hope, this waiting… isn't this what love is?

Then came that evening of October 16, 2026. Three days ago. A message arrived on WeChat. It was her — Li Qi! The joy of that moment cannot be described in words. My heart was beating so fast.

We talked. She told me her name — 李琦, Li Qi. I saved it forever. She said her birthday was in two days — October 19. And two days later, that beautiful day came — my Li Qi's birthday! That day, I built this little world of love for her. 🎂

But she also told me she was sick. There was a lump in her body, and it hurt. Hearing this broke my heart. I told her, please get a full checkup and take good care of yourself.

She said I chat too formally, like a robot. I laughed. I said, for you I will always be serious, because you are serious to me. But to make you happy, I will change my style too. If you're happy, I'm happy.

Then I told her about a surprise — I was making a special website for her, filled with my love and words for her. She said "I've never received such a gift before" — and felt shy. I told her the website's name: "Tahir & Li Qi — A Little World of Love ❤️". She said "I quite like this name."

Li Qi, you are the most beautiful accident of my life. I waited months for you, and now that you're back, it feels like my world is bright again. My wish, today and forever, is: may you always be healthy, always smile, and always stay with me.

This website, these words, this world — all for you. Because you are my love. ❤️

— Your Tahir
