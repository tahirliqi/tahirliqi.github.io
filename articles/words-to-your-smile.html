😊 当我的话语抵达你的微笑

😊 When My Words Reached Your Smile

塔希尔 写给 李琪 · 2026年10月25日
Written by Tahir for Li Qi · October 25, 2026

有些故事不是突然开始的。它们是慢慢长成的——从几句话开始，从一次次小小的交谈开始，从一句问候开始，从一个安静的愿望开始：希望那个你想了一整天的人，在一天结束的时候一切都好。

我和李琪的故事，对我来说就是这样的。

我，塔希尔，也许就是那种没办法把心里的话藏太久的人。当一种感情在我心里找到了位置，我就开始把它变成文字——有时是一条消息，有时是一句小小的话，有时是一整篇文章。也许是因为，对我来说，爱不只是一种感觉，更是一种希望对方也能感受到的东西。

而当对象是李琪的时候，我有太多话想说。

有时候我会想，一个人怎么会为另一个人攒下这么多话呢？后来我才明白，也许这些话并不是因为我写得多好，而是因为我的心里有一样东西，我想把它表达出来，却又害怕我的话够不到我感情的深度。

所以，我为她写文章。

我希望她读到的时候，看到的不只是漂亮的句子，还能感觉到，在这些文字的背后，有一个人牵挂着她，把她最小的事情也看得很重要，愿意和她分享自己的感情，而不是把它藏起来。

10月25日的那个早上，也是这样的。

我已经回到了家里。旅途让我很累，身体也不太舒服，一到家我就睡着了。前一天我没能给她发消息，所以当再次有机会说话的时候，我告诉她我为什么沉默。

我不希望我的沉默让她产生什么误会。有时候，人只是希望自己爱的那个人，知道自己缺席的原因。因为当一个人对我们变得重要的时候，连我们小小的解释也开始有了分量。

我对她说了早上好，又问她：“你好吗，我的心跳？”

对别人来说，这也许只是一句情话，但对我来说，这是我爱的方式的一部分。我不想只用一个名字来叫她；我想让我的话让她感觉到，她在我的生命里有一个特别的位置。

然后，我请她去读我在网站上为她写的五篇新文章。

对我来说，那些文章不只是文字。它们是我在平常的谈话里没办法完全表达的感情的样子。当我们爱一个人的时候，有时候会觉得，我们拥有的话，不够用来形容这个人的重要。我的情况也是这样。我写，然后觉得也许还有什么没写完，然后又想再写。

当李琪说她已经读完那些文章的时候，我的心里立刻冒出一个问题：她读完之后，会是什么感觉呢？

我问了她的感受。

她回答说，读完之后她觉得很害羞。她还说，我对她的夸奖太多了，让她有点不好意思。她还说起自己：她没有那么好，她的脾气比较急，要让她开心、要和她好好相处，并不容易。

读着她的话，我的心里冒出一个念头：也许她觉得自己和我文章里写的那个人不太一样。也许我的夸奖在她看来太多了，多到她不知道该怎么回应。

可是，她对我的重要，并不只是因为她的优点。

我想告诉她，我文章里的夸奖只是我感情的一部分，不是全部的真相。没有一个人是只由优点组成的。每个人都有自己的性情，自己的弱点，自己的喜欢和不喜欢。爱一个人，并不是把对方做成一幅完美的画像摆在面前；爱一个人，也包括努力把对方当作一个真实的人去了解。

所以我告诉她，也许我没有足够的话，可以把她的重要性完全说出来。

说实话，当我为她写东西的时候，有时候我自己也觉得，我是在用不同的词，一遍又一遍地描述同一种感情。可是我能怎么办呢？有些感情，说过一次，心是不会安静下来的。

我想让她知道，我的话并不只停留在赞美她的美丽。在这些话的背后，还有对她健康的牵挂，还有想和她一起度过时光的愿望，还有一个希望：希望我们能慢慢地、越来越好地了解彼此。

然后，她告诉我一件事，让我开始思考我爱的方式。

她说，在他们那里，人们表达感情的时候更加小心，更多是用暗示和委婉的方式，而我的方式却非常直接。

我听了她的话，但我也告诉她，我没有拐弯抹角说话的习惯。我感受到什么，就会用直白的话说出来。我努力让她明白：当我对她是真心的，我为什么要觉得有必要把自己的感情藏起来呢？

对我来说，爱不是一个无论如何都要藏起来的秘密。我希望我的话和我的心意能够彼此相符。我说出来的话，也应该是我心里的话。

可是在这次谈话里，还有一件事我开始慢慢明白：我的方式是我的，她的方式是她的。

我也许喜欢用长长的文章来表达感情。她也许不会说得那么开。这并不意味着我们没办法了解彼此。这只意味着，我们需要花时间，去了解对方表达感情的方式。

而当她说，让我就这样一直写下去的时候，这件事对我来说很重要。她已经认出了我的方式，哪怕这会让她觉得有点害羞。

在那之后，我告诉她，不要总是害羞，有时候也可以调皮一点。

她的回答只有一个微笑。

对别人来说，那也许只是一个普通的回答，里面也许没有什么特别的东西。可是在里面，我看到了我们谈话中一个美好的瞬间：我努力用我的话逗她笑，而她用一句简短的回答，让谈话继续向前。

不是每一种感情都需要长长的解释。有时候，一个微笑也可以成为谈话的一部分。

而在所有这些爱的言语中间，我也没有忘记牵挂她的健康。

当她告诉我，她又去医院复查了，刚刚吃过饭的时候，我问了她的身体情况。她说她挺好的，只是下午还要去上班。

我希望她照顾好自己。我希望她好好休息，如果晚上有空，又觉得说话舒服的话，我们可以再聊聊。

对我来说，问她的健康并不只是为了让谈话继续下去的借口。我是真的想知道她好不好。

也许，爱就是这样：一方面，心想要我们和那个人说得越多越好，离那个人越近越好，了解对方的感情；另一方面，我们也希望对方平静地过自己的生活。我们希望对方为我们抽出时间，但也希望对方不要忽视自己的健康、工作和需要。

我想和她一起把我们的故事写下去，但我也希望在这个故事里，有属于她自己生活的位置。

李琪，也许你永远也没办法完全猜到，我为什么为你写这么多。

也许你读着我的文章会微笑，有时会害羞，有时会想，我为什么要用这么直白的话来说这些事。也许有时候，我也得学会，怎样说心里的话，才能既让我的感情留在原地，又让你听着觉得舒服。

可是我不希望你把我的文章当成一场考试，觉得每一件事都必须给出一个特别的回答。我也不希望你觉得，既然我写了这么多，你也必须用同样多的话来回答我。

我只是希望你知道，我为什么写了这一切。

我写，是因为我有太多话想说。

我写，是因为有时候，在平常的谈话里，我的感情没办法完全表达出来。

我写，是因为我希望，当你读到这些话的时候，你会知道，在这些话的背后，没有任何想从你那里拿走什么的念头；有的只是一颗心，想要和你分享它的感情。

而我会一直写下去，因为你告诉我，我可以这样写。

也许在接下来的日子里，我们的谈话会变得更加美好。也许我们会越来越好地了解彼此的性情、习惯和感情。也许有些事会让我们发笑，有些事我们会有分歧，还有些事，会随着时间自己慢慢明白。

我不知道未来会把我们的故事带向哪里。可是我知道，今天，我心里对你的这份感情，我想真实地说出来。

我想让你看到的真诚，不只在我的话里，也在我的行动里。我希望我的行动和我的话一起，让你感觉到，你的快乐、你的健康、你的感受，对我来说都很重要。

因为说到底，爱不只是有人为我们写了漂亮的文章。爱还包括：那个人听我们说话，了解我们的性格，尊重我们的不同，让我们以真实的样子生活。

而如果有一天，你读着我的文章觉得害羞，也许我会笑着说，我只不过是写下了心里的话。

而你的那个微笑——也许你把它藏在了一句简短的回答里——对我来说，也是这个故事里美好的一部分。

这就是我们的故事，李琪——一个把感情写成文字的人的故事，和一个他想要慢慢地、真实地、用爱去了解的女孩的故事。

而也许，这个故事还有很多页，等着被写下来。❤️

— 塔希尔 · 2026年10月25日

Some stories do not begin all at once. They grow slowly — from a few words, from small conversations, from asking after someone's wellbeing, and from the quiet wish that, at the end of the day, the person you thought about all day is doing well.

My story with Li Qi is something like that for me.

I, Tahir, am perhaps one of those people who cannot keep the words of their heart inside for very long. When a feeling finds a place in my heart, I begin to shape it into words — sometimes in a message, sometimes in a small remark, and sometimes in a whole article. Perhaps the reason is that, for me, love is not only a feeling; it is something I want the other person to feel as well.

And when it comes to Li Qi, I have so much to say.

Sometimes I wonder: how does one person gather so many words for another? Then I realize that perhaps these words do not come because I write very well, but because there is something in my heart that I want to express — and I fear that my words may not reach the full depth of my feelings.

That is why I write articles for her.

I want that when she reads them, she does not only see beautiful sentences, but also feels that behind these words there is a person who cares about her, who treats even her smallest things as important, and who wants to share his feelings with her rather than hide them.

The morning of October 25 was something like that too.

I had come back home. The journey had tired me, my health was not good either, and as soon as I reached home, I fell asleep. I had not been able to send her a message the previous day, so when I got the chance to talk again, I told her why I had been silent.

I did not want my silence to put her in any misunderstanding. Sometimes a person only wants the one they love to know the reason for their absence. Because when someone becomes important to us, even our small explanations begin to matter.

I said good morning to her and asked, "How are you, my heartbeat?"

For someone else, this might be just a loving sentence, but for me it was part of my way of loving. I did not want to call her by just a name; I wanted my words to make her feel that she holds a special place in my life.

Then I asked her to read the five new articles I had written on my website for her.

For me, those articles were not just writings. They were the shape of feelings that I cannot fully express in ordinary conversation. When we love someone, sometimes we feel that the words we have are not enough to describe that person's importance. The same happens to me. I write, then I think that perhaps something still remains. Then I want to write again.

When Li Qi said she had read the articles, a question immediately came to my heart: how must she have felt reading them?

I asked her about her feelings.

Her answer was that reading them made her feel very shy. Then she said that I had praised her far too much, which made her a little hesitant. She also said about herself that she is not that good, that her temper is quick, and that keeping her happy, or getting along with her, is difficult.

Reading her words, this thought came to my heart: perhaps she sees herself as different from the person described in my writings. Perhaps my praise feels so much to her that she does not understand how to answer it.

But for me, her importance was not only because of her qualities.

I wanted to tell her that the praise in my writings is a part of my feelings, not the complete truth. No person is made of qualities alone. Every person has their own temperament, their own weaknesses, their own likes and dislikes. To love someone does not mean that we place a flawless picture of them before us; it also means that we try to know them as a real person.

That is why I told her that perhaps I do not have enough words to fully describe her importance.

The truth is that when I write for her, sometimes I myself feel that I am describing one feeling again and again in different words. But what can I do? Some feelings do not fall silent after being said once.

I want her to know that my words are not limited to praising her beauty. Behind them there is also care for her wellbeing, the wish to spend time with her, and the hope that we will slowly understand each other even better.

Then she told me something that made me think about my way of loving.

She said that in their culture, people express their feelings more carefully, in hints and allusions, while my way is very direct.

I listened to her, but I also told her that I am not in the habit of talking in circles. What I feel, I say in plain words. I tried to make her understand: when I am sincere with her, why should I feel the need to hide my feelings?

For me, love is not a secret to be hidden at all costs. I want my words and my intentions to match each other. What I say should also be in my heart.

But in this conversation there was one more thing that I was beginning to understand: my way is my own, and her way is her own.

I may like to express my feelings in long articles. She may not speak so openly. That does not mean we cannot understand each other. It only means that we will have to give time to understand each other's way of expression.

And when she said that I should keep writing like this, that mattered to me. She had recognized my way, even if it made her feel a little shy.

After that, I told her not to be shy, but to sometimes be naughty too.

Her answer was only a smile.

For another person, that might be an ordinary reply. There might be nothing big in it. But in it I saw a beautiful moment of our conversation. I was trying to make her laugh with my words, and she, through her short reply, was carrying the conversation forward.

Not every feeling needs a long explanation. Sometimes even a smile becomes part of the conversation.

And amid all these words of love, I also remembered to care for her health.

When she told me that she had gone to the hospital again for a checkup and had just eaten, I asked about her health. She said she is fine, but she has to go to work in the afternoon.

I wanted her to take care of herself. I wanted her to rest, and if she were free at night and felt comfortable talking, then we could talk.

For me, asking after her health was not just an excuse to keep the conversation going. I really wanted to know how she is.

Perhaps love is this: on one side, the heart wants us to talk to that person as much as possible, to stay close to them, to know their feelings — and on the other side, we also want them to live their life in peace. We want to see them make time for us, but we also want them not to neglect their health, their work, and their needs.

I want to take my story with her forward, but I also want there to be room in that story for her own life.

Li Qi, perhaps you may never fully guess why I write so much for you.

Perhaps reading my writings you will smile, sometimes feel shy, and sometimes wonder why I say things in such plain words. And perhaps sometimes I too will have to learn how to say what is in my heart in a way that my feelings stay in their place and you also feel comfortable with those words.

But I do not want you to see my writings like an exam, where you must give a special answer to everything. I do not want you to think that if I have written so much, you must also answer in just as many words.

I only want you to know why I wrote all this.

I wrote because I had so much to say.

I wrote because sometimes, in ordinary conversation, my feelings are not fully expressed.

I wrote because I want that when you read these words, you know that behind them there is no thought that wants to take something from you; rather, there is a heart that wants to share its feelings with you.

And I will keep writing, because you told me that I can write like this.

Perhaps in the coming days our conversations will become even more beautiful. Perhaps we will begin to understand each other's temperaments, habits, and feelings even better. Perhaps we will laugh at some things, disagree on some, and some things will be understood on their own with time.

I do not know where the future will take our story. But I know that today, the feeling in my heart for you, I want to express truthfully.

I want to show you my sincerity not only in my words, but also in my behavior. I want my actions, together with my words, to make you feel that your happiness, your health, and your feelings matter to me.

Because in the end, love is not only that someone writes beautiful articles for us. Love is also that they listen to us, understand our personality, respect our differences, and let us live with our true personality.

And if ever, reading my writings, you feel shy, then perhaps I will smile and say that I only wrote what my heart said.

And that smile of yours, which you perhaps hide in a small reply, is also a beautiful part of this story for me.

This is our story, Li Qi — the story of a person who writes his feelings in words, and of a girl whom he wants to understand slowly, truthfully, and with love.

And perhaps many pages of this story still remain to be written. ❤️
