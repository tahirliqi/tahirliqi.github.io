🌹 我爱你的一千个理由

🌹 A Thousand Reasons I Love You

塔希尔 写给 李琪 · 2026年10月19日
Written by Tahir for Li Qi · October 19, 2026

如果有人问我为什么爱你，我可以说出一千个理由。但其实，第一个理由就已经足够了——因为你就是你。

我爱你的笑容。那不是普通的笑，是能让整个冬天都温暖起来的笑。每当我累了、难过了，只要想起你的笑容，全世界都亮了。

我爱你的善良。你对每一个人都那么温柔，连陌生人都能感受到你的温暖。在这个冷漠的世界里，你像一束光，照亮你走过的每一条路。

我爱你的勇敢。一个女孩，在深夜的街道上也能笑得那么灿烂，那么自由。你的快乐是真实的，不伪装，不做作。

我爱你说话的样子，爱你走路的样子，爱你认真时的样子，甚至爱你生气时撅起嘴的样子。你的每一个样子，我都爱。

有人说，爱一个人需要理由。但我觉得，真正的爱不需要理由。如果非要说一个理由，那就是——我的心选择了你，它不听我的话，它只听你的。

一千个理由，一万个理由，都比不上一个事实：我爱你，李琪。不是因为你完美，而是因为你就是你。

💖 这一千个理由，我要用一辈子向你证明。💖

If someone asked me why I love you, I could give a thousand reasons. But honestly, the first one is already enough — because you are you.

I love your smile. It's not an ordinary smile — it's the kind that can warm up an entire winter. Whenever I'm tired or sad, just thinking of your smile lights up my whole world.

I love your kindness. You're so gentle with everyone; even strangers can feel your warmth. In this cold world, you're like a beam of light, brightening every road you walk.

I love your courage. A girl who can laugh so brightly, so freely, on a street late at night. Your happiness is real — no pretense, no act.

I love the way you talk, the way you walk, the way you look when you're serious — I even love the way you pout when you're upset. Every version of you, I love.

They say loving someone needs a reason. But I believe true love needs no reason. And if I must give one, it's this — my heart chose you. It doesn't listen to me; it only listens to you.

A thousand reasons, ten thousand reasons — none compare to one simple fact: I love you, Li Qi. Not because you're perfect, but because you are you.

💖 These thousand reasons — I will spend my lifetime proving them to you. 💖
