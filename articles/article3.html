⭐ 我写在星星上的承诺

⭐ My Promise, Written in the Stars

塔希尔 写给 李琪 · 2026年10月19日
Written by Tahir for Li Qi · October 19, 2026

今天晚上，我看着天上的星星，一颗一颗地数着，然后我把我的承诺，一句一句地写在了星星上。这样，即使有一天我不在你身边，星星也会替我守护你。

我承诺，无论未来有多少风雨，我都会牵着你的手，一起走下去。顺境的时候，我和你一起笑；逆境的时候，我为你遮风挡雨。

我承诺，我会学好中文，只为能更懂你的心。我想听懂你的每一句话，想走进你的每一个梦。语言不应该成为我们之间的墙，而应该成为我们爱情的桥。

我承诺，我会努力工作，给你一个温暖的家。不一定是多大的房子，但一定是一个充满爱的地方，一个你每次回家都会微笑的地方。

我承诺，我会尊重你、珍惜你、保护你。你不是我的所有物，你是我的爱人，我的伙伴，我的灵魂伴侣。我会用一生去证明，你选择我是值得的。

李琪，我来自很远的地方，巴基斯坦。我们的文化不同，语言不同，但爱是相同的。今晚的星星可以作证——我的心，从遇见你的那一刻起，就只属于你一个人。

如果有一天你老了，头发白了，走不动了，我还会牵着你的手，像今天一样，看着星星，告诉你：李琪，我爱你，从过去，到现在，到永远。

💖 星星为证，我心永恒。💖

Tonight, I looked at the stars, counting them one by one, and I wrote my promises on them, sentence by sentence. That way, even if one day I'm not by your side, the stars will guard you for me.

I promise: no matter how many storms the future brings, I will hold your hand and walk on together. In good times, I'll laugh with you; in hard times, I'll shield you from the wind and rain.

I promise: I will learn Chinese well, just so I can understand your heart better. I want to understand every word you say, to walk into every dream you dream. Language should never be a wall between us — it should be a bridge for our love.

I promise: I will work hard to give you a warm home. Maybe not a big house, but a place full of love — a place where you'll smile every time you come home.

I promise: I will respect you, cherish you, protect you. You are not my possession — you are my lover, my partner, my soulmate. I will spend my life proving that choosing me was worth it.

Li Qi, I come from far away — Pakistan. Our cultures are different, our languages are different, but love is the same. Tonight's stars bear witness — my heart, from the moment I met you, belongs to you alone.

If one day you're old, your hair is white, and you can't walk anymore, I'll still hold your hand, just like today, look at the stars, and tell you: Li Qi, I love you — from the past, to the present, to forever.

💖 The stars bear witness; my heart is eternal. 💖
