🌙 你对我微笑的那一天

🌙 The Day You Smiled at Me

塔希尔 写给 李琪 · 2026年10月19日
Written by Tahir for Li Qi · October 19, 2026

我还记得那一天。夜晚的街道上灯火闪烁，你站在那里，长发随风轻轻飘动，然后你笑了——那一笑，像一道光，照进了我心里最深的地方。

在遇见你之前，我的生活是黑白的。我每天工作、吃饭、睡觉，像一台机器一样重复着同样的日子。我以为这就是人生，直到你出现。

你的笑容有一种神奇的力量。它让我忘记了所有的烦恼，忘记了异国他乡的孤独。那一刻，我听见自己的心跳得那么快，我知道，有些事情永远改变了。

有人说，爱情是两个灵魂的相遇。我不懂那些深奥的道理，我只知道，从那一天起，我的每一个梦里都有你，我的每一个计划里都有你。

李琪，你可能不知道，你的一个微笑，拯救了一个在异乡漂泊的心。从那天起，我不再是一个人了，因为我的心里住进了一个叫李琪的女孩。

💖 谢谢你，对我微笑的那一天。💖

I still remember that day. The street lights were flickering at night, you stood there, your long hair dancing softly in the wind, and then you smiled — that smile was like a beam of light, shining into the deepest part of my heart.

Before I met you, my life was black and white. I worked, ate, and slept every day, repeating the same routine like a machine. I thought that was life, until you appeared.

Your smile has a magical power. It made me forget all my worries, forget the loneliness of being far from home. In that moment, I heard my own heart beating so fast, and I knew something had changed forever.

They say love is the meeting of two souls. I don't understand such deep philosophy — I only know that from that day on, you have been in every dream of mine, in every plan I make.

Li Qi, you may not know this, but your one smile saved a heart wandering in a foreign land. From that day on, I am no longer alone, because a girl named Li Qi now lives in my heart.

💖 Thank you — for smiling at me that day. 💖
